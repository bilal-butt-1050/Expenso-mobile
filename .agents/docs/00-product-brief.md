# Product Brief — Expenso Mobile

## Product Goal
Deliver an instant, frictionless personal finance mobile app for iOS and Android. Replaces spreadsheets with clean dark-mode visuals, thumb-reachable logging, and one-tap paid/unpaid expense toggles.

## Core Navigation Structure
1. **Auth Stack**: Login, Registration, OTP Verification modal, Google Sign-In.
2. **Main Tabs (Elevated Floating Dock)**:
   - **Home (Dashboard)**: Financial metrics (Current Balance, Projected Balance, Unpaid totals, 12-month visual charts).
   - **Expenses**: Infinite scrolling list of expenses with one-tap Paid/Unpaid status pills, filter bars (Month, Category, Status), and floating "+" action button.
   - **Budget**: Category spending progress bars (spent vs limit) with alert badges for over-budget categories.
   - **Loans**: Debts tracking (Money Lent vs Money Borrowed), partial settlement sheets, due dates.
   - **Settings**: Income management, category color/icon editor, currency settings, logout.
